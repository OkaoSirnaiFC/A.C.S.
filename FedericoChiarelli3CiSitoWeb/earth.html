Earth: The Black Bile

Earth was one of the four classical elements in ancient Greek philosophy, associated with heaviness, stability, matter, and the physical world. Empedocles included earth among the four fundamental elements of the cosmos, while Plato associated it with the cube because of its solidity and stability. Aristotle considered earth the heaviest element and believed it naturally moved toward the center of the universe. Earth was also linked to fertility, agriculture, and the underworld through deities such as Demeter, Persephone, and Hades. In ancient medicine, it was connected to black bile and the melancholic temperament, as well as autumn, dryness, and coldness. In alchemy, earth symbolized solidity and permanence, and was associated with salt and a downward-pointing triangle crossed by a horizontal line.
