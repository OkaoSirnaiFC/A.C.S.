Mercury: The Spirit

It is associated with the feminine and passive aspect, complementary to sulfur. It symbolizes intellect, imagination, and higher mental faculties, and its properties are flexibility and adaptability. Mercury is associated with the principle of fusibility and volatility, and is connected to the elements of water and air, as well as the Moon. Alchemists believed that mercury could create a bridge between the body (salt) and the soul (sulfur), transcending the liquid and solid states, as well as life and death.
