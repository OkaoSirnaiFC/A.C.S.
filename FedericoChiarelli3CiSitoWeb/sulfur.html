Sulfur: The Soul

It is associated with the masculine and active aspect; it symbolizes consciousness and the expansion of thought, and its properties are dryness, heat, expansion, and dissolution. Sulfur is associated with the principle of combustibility, as it is connected to the element of fire and the Sun. Sulfur, combined with liquid mercury, was transformed into fiery mercury, also called philosophical mercury, to achieve the alchemical marriage. This term symbolizes the union of opposites, that is, the fusion of the masculine and feminine principles, of the Sun and the Moon, to achieve the "quintessence" through the balance of opposites, in order to purify both matter and the soul, and achieve perfection and spiritual transformation.
