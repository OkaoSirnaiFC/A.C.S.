Fire: The Yellow Bile

Fire was one of the four classical elements in ancient Greek philosophy and symbolized energy, passion, and transformation. The philosopher Heraclitus considered fire the fundamental substance of the universe, believing that all things emerged from and returned to it through constant transformation. He also saw the soul as a mixture of fire and water, with fire representing purity. Plato associated fire with the tetrahedron because of its sharp shape, while Aristotle described fire as hot and dry and believed the elements naturally moved within the structure of the cosmos. In ancient medicine, fire was linked to yellow bile, the choleric temperament, and summer. In alchemy, it was represented by an upward-pointing triangle and associated with sulfur and transformation.
