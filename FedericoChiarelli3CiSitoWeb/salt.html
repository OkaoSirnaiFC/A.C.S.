Salt: The Body

It is associated with the stable and solid aspect of the alchemical triad, complementary to the soul and spiritual aspects. It is the neutral element, the mediator between the masculine, active aspect of sulfur and the feminine, passive aspect of mercury, which cancels their opposites and creates balance. It symbolizes wisdom and knowledge; and has properties of condensation, sedimentation, and crystallization. It is associated with the principle of incombustibility and non-volatility and is connected to the element of earth.
